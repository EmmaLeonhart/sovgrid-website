Solar for Canadian data centres

Clean power for the data centres Canada is building.

SovGrid builds solar energy for Canadian data centres, so they can grow without drawing down the local power grid or turning to natural gas.

The problem

Data centres need a lot of electricity, around the clock, and demand keeps rising.

Strained local grids

A large new data centre can draw as much power as a town. That load lands on grids that also serve homes and businesses.

Gas as the stopgap

When the grid can't keep up, the quick fix is often on-site natural gas generation, which adds emissions for as long as it runs.

Growth on hold

Waiting for grid connections and capacity slows down projects that communities and businesses are counting on.

What we do

We develop solar generation built around the needs of a data centre, so the facility brings its own clean power instead of taking it from its neighbours.
